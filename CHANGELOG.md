# Changelog

## v0.9.0
The game is now Goat Records (previously Side A: Label Empire), and the top rank is Greatest of All Time. Label ranks replace fame-only tiers: six ranks with listed goals for fame, releases, facilities, chart peak, awards and cash, promoted at the weekly settlement. Albums take one or two discovered themes; genre + theme fit is revealed on recording, combos level up with releases and lose Reach when repeated within 26 weeks. New Headquarters tab with a pixel-art lot of ten facilities, upgrades, moves and seven neighbor pairings; artists appear where they work. Rank-up banners, combo notebook and headquarters takings in Finances. Saves from 0.8 keep the rank their fame had earned. Source reorganized into src/, styles/ and tests/ with balance tables in src/data/.

## v0.8
Cap tour decisions at three per label week across all active tours. Choices schedule multi-day review windows based on recovery, promotions and routing. Add fast-forward through quiet days with weekly/album stops, fair shared tour scheduling and saved review dates. Keep daily financial and energy settlement; close final dates automatically when no review fits the budget.

## v0.7
Correct tour prompt timing, show-counted ticket boosts, scheduled recovery energy and nonrecursive rest events. Add varied rotating scenarios and final-day wrap-ups. Unify ticket forecasts with daily settlement, rebalance gate revenue and event fees, and show projected tour finish/break-even. Preserve prior tour ledgers and refresh old unresolved prompts.

## v0.6
Artist inspiration resets after each release, recovers between projects, and gates new recording at 25. Thresholds at 50/75/90 improve production outcomes, with a locked recording snapshot and visible meter/calculation contributions. Existing projects keep their original rules.

## v0.5.1
Active activities dashboard with direct project/event/tour navigation, smaller header, active tours before bookings, compact expandable album rows and collapsed finished-tour history. Save format and gameplay rules unchanged.

## v0.5
Top 300 competition, six rival labels, varied CEO encounters, ten career levels, daily tour decisions and annual genre awards. Includes save migration and 62 simulation checks plus three interface harnesses.

## v0.4
Ranked rooms, album add-ons, Star Producer, expectations, morale, touring and career continuation.

## v0.3
Paid scouting, staff candidate recruitment, artist contracts, chart milestones and album campaign events.

## v0.2
Five interactive album stages, contribution animations, final renaming, reshuffles and release strategies.

## v0.1
Initial label management, artists, staff, production, genres and industry events.

Versions 0.1–0.8 were released as Side A: Label Empire from the side-a-label-empire repository.
