# Roadmap

## 0.9 — label growth (done)

Label ranks, album themes and combos, headquarters facilities.

## 1.0 — the lot (done)

The headquarters lot as the main screen with a live clock; a 20-year campaign with yearly goals and New Game+; recording locations and paid gigs; hot streaks, lead singles and ad campaigns.

## Next

1. **Fewer interruptions.** Batch minor events and offer a setting to auto-resolve with the safe choice.
2. **Fans at the lot.** Visitors on the street that grow with fame, and fan attractions (merch stand, listening booth) as street decor.
3. **Awards night.** An animated ceremony for the annual awards instead of a results card.
4. **Roster and scouting legacy bonuses.** Hooks for an extra roster slot and cheaper scouting in New Game+.
5. **Gig income in Finances.** Show gig pay and ad spend in the weekly settlement history.

## Open questions
