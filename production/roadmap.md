# Roadmap

## 0.9 — label growth (done)

Label ranks, album themes and combos, headquarters facilities. Source split into `src/`, data tables in `src/data/`.

## Next

1. **Long campaign.** Twenty operating years with a final rank target, then a new game with a small carry-over bonus based on the last run.
2. **Recording locations.** Rented spots (church hall, barn, beach house) separate from owned rooms. They level up with use, and some unlock themes.
3. **Paid gigs as training.** Some development sessions earn money and can turn up themes.
4. **Hot streaks.** A visible glow on artists who are on a run, with the bonus spelled out.
5. **Singles and ad types.** Release singles before an album. Rank unlocks radio, playlist, billboard and TV pushes.
6. **Fewer interruptions.** Batch events, plus a setting to auto-resolve with the safe choice.
